# Changelog

## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-06

First App Store release.

- Report timeline: the last hour label no longer truncates to "…"
- Trends: the week and month windows are the last 7 and 30 calendar nights, so a night you didn't record shows as an empty slot and counts in "N of M nights recorded"
- Trends: a date axis with readable labels in both the week and month views

## [0.1.0] - 2026-10-06

First public release.

- Overnight snore tracking that keeps working with the screen locked: YAMNet runs CPU-only on the device
- Morning report: when and how much you snored, intensity relative to the room, and playback of snore clips
- History with trends across nights
- Nightly bedtime reminder
- Onboarding and a sensitivity setting (Medium or High)
- Everything stays on the phone: no accounts, no analytics, no network
