# Privacy Policy — Snore Laboratory

_Last updated: 20 August 2026_

Snore Laboratory is a snore tracker that runs entirely on your iPhone.

## The short version

We do not collect anything. There is no account, no server, and the app makes
no network requests of any kind. Nothing you record leaves your device unless
you choose to share it yourself.

## What the app records, and where it stays

- **Microphone audio.** While a sleep session is running, the app listens
  through your iPhone's microphone and analyzes the sound on the device to
  detect snoring. Whole-night audio is never written to disk — it exists only
  momentarily in memory while being analyzed.
- **Snore clips.** When the app confirms a snoring episode, it saves a short
  clip (about 12 seconds) so you can play it back. These clips are stored in
  the app's private container, are **excluded from iCloud and iTunes backups**,
  and are deleted automatically after 90 days.
- **Session metrics.** Start and end times, detected episodes, loudness
  measurements, and your settings are stored in a database inside the app's
  private container. This database is included in your normal device backups,
  so your history survives getting a new phone.

## What we do not do

- No data is transmitted anywhere. The app contains no networking code.
- No analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs are included.
- No accounts, no sign-in, no identifiers are created or shared.
- Your data is never sold or disclosed, because we never receive it.

## Your control over your data

Settings → **Delete all data** permanently erases every recorded night,
episode, and audio clip from your device. Deleting the app removes everything
it stored.

Microphone access can be revoked at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy &
Security → Microphone. Without it, the app cannot record sessions.

## Crash reports

If you have opted in to sharing analytics with Apple (iOS Settings → Privacy &
Security → Analytics & Improvements), Apple may send us crash reports. These
are collected and anonymized by Apple, contain no audio and no recorded data,
and are governed by Apple's privacy policy.

## Children

Snore Laboratory is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

## Not a medical device

Snore Laboratory is not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat, or
monitor any medical condition, including sleep apnea. If you are concerned
about your sleep or breathing, talk to a physician.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new
date. Since the app collects nothing, any change would be a clarification
rather than a new use of data.
